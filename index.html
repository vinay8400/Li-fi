01 / The principle

Communication,
at the speed of light.

Li-Fi is a form of wireless communication that uses light instead of radio waves. In this prototype, a changing audio signal controls an LED too quickly for the eye to notice.

The light carries that change through free space. A solar panel converts the received light back into an electrical signal, which is amplified and played by a compact speaker.

THE LINKLight → Electricity → Sound

03 / Characteristics

Designed around
the optical link.

The project is a hands-on exploration of transmitting sound through changing visible light.

01

Visible light
communication

Information moves on a light beam rather than a conventional RF link.

02

Audio

Solar panel

A photovoltaic panel turns received light back into electricity.

The communication path is based on light, not a radio transmission.

Light-based

06

Energy-aware
receiving

The receiver uses a solar-panel sensing element.

+

Advantages

  • Uses light for communication
  • Can coexist with lighting infrastructure
  • Good directional communication
  • Useful for indoor optical links
  • Broad electromagnetic spectrum potential
−

Considerations

  • Needs a suitable light path
  • Physical obstructions can interrupt the link
  • Receiver alignment can matter
  • Lighting conditions affect performance
  • Ambient outdoor light can affect reception
